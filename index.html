QUESTIONS

Do you manage PGs and hostels of all sizes?

Yes. Manpower rosters and scope are finalized after a joint assessment of your site, shifts, amenities and requirements.

How is the fee structured?

Pricing is based on the final scope, approved staffing plan and commercial schedule. Contact us for a tailored proposal after a free site assessment.

Salaries of deployed manpower, agency bills, utilities, AMC/OEM and spare parts, major repairs and government/insurance charges are discussed and listed clearly as pass-through items.

Yes: daily, weekly, monthly and quarterly reporting, including a monthly MIS with KPI dashboard and action tracker.
